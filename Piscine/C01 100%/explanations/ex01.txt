Ex01 – ft_ultimate_ft

Goal (in simple words):
Set an integer to 42 using a chain of pointers.

What you are expected to do:
- The function receives a pointer to a pointer ... 9 levels deep.
- It must set the final int to 42.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) Use *********nbr to reach the int.
2) Assign 42 to it.

Why it works:
- Each * follows one level of indirection until you reach the actual int.

Beginner tips:
- Count the * carefully (9 stars).
- Do not print anything.
