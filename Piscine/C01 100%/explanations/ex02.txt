Ex02 – ft_swap

Goal (in simple words):
Swap the values of two integers using pointers.

What you are expected to do:
- The function receives two int pointers.
- After the function, their values are exchanged.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) Store *a in a temporary variable.
2) Set *a to *b.
3) Set *b to the temporary value.

Why it works:
- The temp variable prevents losing one value during the swap.

Beginner tips:
- You must use a temporary variable.
- Do not print anything.
