Ex03 – ft_div_mod

Goal (in simple words):
Compute division and modulo of two integers.

What you are expected to do:
- The function receives a and b, plus pointers div and mod.
- Store a / b in *div and a % b in *mod.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) *div = a / b;
2) *mod = a % b;

Why it works:
- / gives the quotient, % gives the remainder.

Beginner tips:
- Assume b is not 0 (per subject).
- Do not print anything.
