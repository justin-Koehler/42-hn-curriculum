Ex04 – ft_ultimate_div_mod

Goal (in simple words):
Replace a with a / b and b with a % b.

What you are expected to do:
- The function receives pointers to a and b.
- After the function, *a is the quotient, *b is the remainder.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) Save *a in a temp variable.
2) Compute *a = *a / *b.
3) Compute *b = temp % *b.

Why it works:
- You must keep the original a before overwriting it.

Beginner tips:
- Use a temp variable or you lose the original a.
- Do not print anything.
