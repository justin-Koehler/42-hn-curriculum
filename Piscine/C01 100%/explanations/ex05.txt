Ex05 – ft_putstr

Goal (in simple words):
Print a string (a sequence of characters).

What you are expected to do:
- The function receives a char *str.
- Print every character until you reach '\0'.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) Use an index i = 0.
2) While str[i] is not '\0', write str[i].
3) Increment i each time.

Why it works:
- In C, strings end with a null character '\0'.

Beginner tips:
- Print one character at a time with write.
- Do not add extra spaces or newlines.
