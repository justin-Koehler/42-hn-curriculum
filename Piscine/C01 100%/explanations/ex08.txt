Ex08 – ft_sort_int_tab

Goal (in simple words):
Sort an array of integers in ascending order.

What you are expected to do:
- The function receives an int array and its size.
- After the function, the array is sorted from smallest to largest.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) Use a simple sorting algorithm (like bubble sort).
2) Compare elements and swap if out of order.
3) Repeat until no swaps are needed.

Why it works:
- Bubble sort repeatedly pushes the largest values to the end.

Beginner tips:
- Keep it simple; efficiency is not required here.
- Do not print anything.
