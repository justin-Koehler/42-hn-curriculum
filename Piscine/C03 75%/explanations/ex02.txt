Ex02 – ft_strcat

Goal (in simple words):
Append src to the end of dest.

What you are expected to do:
- Find the end of dest.
- Copy src starting there.
- Return dest.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) Move i to the end of dest (dest[i] == '\0').
2) Copy src[j] to dest[i] and increment both.
3) Copy the final '\0'.

Why it works:
- The '\0' marks where to start appending.
