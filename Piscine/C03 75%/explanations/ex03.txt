Ex03 – ft_strncat

Goal (in simple words):
Append at most n characters from src to dest.

What you are expected to do:
- Find the end of dest.
- Copy up to n characters from src.
- Add '\0' at the end.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) Move i to end of dest.
2) Copy while j < n and src[j] != '\0'.
3) Finish with '\0'.

Why it works:
- It prevents copying more than n characters.
