Ex02 – ft_putnbr

Goal (in simple words):
Print an integer number.

What you are expected to do:
- Handle negative numbers.
- Print digits from left to right.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) If nb is negative, print '-' and make nb positive.
2) If nb is >= 10, print nb / 10 first (recursion or loop).
3) Print the last digit: nb % 10.

Why it works:
- Printing higher digits first ensures correct order.
