Ex03 – ft_atoi

Goal (in simple words):
Convert a string to an integer.

What you are expected to do:
- Skip whitespace.
- Read optional '+' or '-' signs.
- Read digits and build the number.

How to write it (step-by-step):
1) Skip spaces and control characters.
2) Read signs; count how many '-'.
3) Build the number while the char is a digit.
4) Apply sign and return.

Why it works:
- It follows the standard atoi behavior.
